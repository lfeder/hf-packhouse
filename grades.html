How it is set

  • One floor level, one truck level. The floors, the pad between the buildings (bin wash and lane) and the ground west and north of them sit at 0.00. The truck court east of them sits at −4.00 at the wall, rising 1% to the east so water runs back to a trench drain along the wall and a trailer sits tipped slightly toward its dock. All four docks (packhouse D1 and D2, fert D1 for containers and D2 for the box truck) open onto it.
  • Retaining wall on the east wall line, 4 ft. Along the buildings it is their foundation: the east wall's footing goes 4 ft deeper. Freestanding wall is needed only across the 50 ft gap between the buildings, past the fert building's south-east corner and beside the top of the ramp, about 75 ft in all. It needs a guard rail on top wherever people walk the pad above.
  • Ramp at the north end, 8%. 50 ft from the pad down to the court, 30 ft wide, east of the packhouse's R2 box-truck apron so the two stay apart. The ramp and the court's east and south edges finish in 2:1 earth banks (a foot down for every 2 ft across) rather than walls. Grade falls to the south, so the topo may show the court meets existing ground there and trucks can come in level from the south or east, with no ramp at all.
  • Doors on the east wall. The two P1 personnel doors open 4 ft above the court, so each gets a landing and steps down. The packhouse's could move to the north wall beside R2 instead; the fert building's has no other wall free in dry storage now that the racks run the north wall.
  • At grade: the packhouse's R1 and R2 and the staff door P4 on the north wall, R3 and P3 onto the bin wash, the fert building's R1 on the lane, and its south-wall doors. Personnel sills sit 2–6 in above the paving outside; roll-ups get a flush threshold with the paving falling away.
  • Container beds sit high. A container on a chassis can stand taller than a 48 in dock truck, and the edge-of-dock leveller only bridges about ±5 in. Confirm the 4 ft drop with the dock vendor (Dock detail).
  • Next: existing grade. Fitting the Pattison topo (1 ft contours, 2025-12-03) to this plan gives existing grade under every part of it, and from that the cut and fill, where the court daylights, and how long the ramp and banks really are.
