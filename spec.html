Amps take a 0.9 power factor. The panel sizes add 25% for continuous loads and leave room for growth. 480 V suits the reefer plugs and the larger motors; a transformer steps down to 120/208 V for receptacles and lighting. At 120/208 V the same load needs about 480 A, so an 800 A service. Both buildings never run everything at once, so the real peak demand is likely nearer 125 kW. The utility and the electrician set the final size.

Lettuce GH, for reference

Not part of the service above. The spec asks whether solar and batteries on the fert building could feed the Lettuce GH, so this is the load they would be up against. From the HELCO bills booked to Lettuce over the 12 months October 2025 to September 2026, at an assumed blended rate, it uses about 305,000 kWh a year, or 35 kW on average. Peak demand is likely around 70 kW, since its fans and pumps run in the day. This is bill spend converted at an assumed rate, not a meter reading: a HELCO bill gives the actual kWh and peak kW.
