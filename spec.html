Our reefer plugs are 240 V 3-phase, so the service is sized at 240 V: one voltage runs the reefers and the motors, with 120 V for receptacles and lighting. Amps take a 0.9 power factor, and the panel sizes add 25% for continuous loads and room for growth. The alternative is a 480/277 V service, about 209 A, so 400 A, with a transformer down to 240 V for the reefers. That means smaller wire, but a transformer to buy. Both buildings never run everything at once, so the real peak demand is likely nearer 125 kW. The utility and the electrician set the final size and the voltage HELCO can offer here.
