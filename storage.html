How these are worked out

  • The expansion is 5.5 acres at Lalamilo on top of today's 12 (JTL 8, BIP 4). Cucumber burn and cooler cases scale by 17.5 ÷ 12. Today's fertiliser burn already covers the nurseries (taken as 0.25 acre) and lettuce (taken as 1 acre), 13.25 acres in all, so fertiliser scales by 18.75 ÷ 13.25.
  • Supplies need is three months of burn after the expansion. Containers are unloaded a pallet at a time straight into storage, so no room is set aside to stage a whole container.
  • Cold storage need is the cooler on its worst day after the expansion. Each order's cases are in the cooler from their pack date to the order's invoice date, when they leave by container, truck or customer pickup, so pickups free space the day they go. A day's count takes everything packed that day and everything leaving it together, its worst moment. Cases are stored in bulk stacks by product and built into pallets as orders go, so positions are cases ÷ 78, a full stack with its top layer. Partial stacks, a few per day, are not counted.
  • Cooler cases come from the pack records (sales_po_fulfillment) for 2025 on, which reconcile with invoices at 99%; earlier years do not. Records packed after their invoice date, or more than a week before it, are left out as entry errors. Query in scripts/cooler-occupancy.sql, run 2026-10-05.
  • Lettuce boxes, trays and film stay at the lettuce packhouse and are not counted here.
  • Burn and on-hand come from the inventory system (weekly burn per item, last counts dated 2026-08-23 to 2026-09-14). A month is 4.345 weeks. Calcium nitrate's burn runs about 65% above what was received over the last 12 months, so fert need may be high.
  • Pallet sizes are each item's own pallet or order unit in the system, e.g. 2,420 lb of calcium nitrate, 500 Keiki boxes, 64 cases of 1,000 bags.
  • Dry storage is racked two high on the floor plus one shelf level: three pallets per position. 8 rack lines × 6 bays × 2 pallets × 3 = 288, plus 2 more bays where the north-wall line runs on to the east wall: 300.
  • Fert room is block-stacked two high: 88 pallets.
  • Cooler is floor-stacked: 60 positions.
  • Dry storage holds cucumber boxes and bags only. Growing supplies stay where they are; coir slabs stay outdoors.
  • Left out as small, under a pallet a month each: labels, chemicals, food-safety kits, bagged micronutrients, clips and twine. They go on shelving.
